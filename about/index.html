💫 Our sweet journey

From a home kitchen to Nairobi's favourite cupcake destination — we’ve grown, but our recipe for love remains the same.

2019

The First Batch

Started as a small home bakery, baking classic vanilla and chocolate cupcakes for neighbours & friends. Word spread fast.

2021

Citywide Love

We expanded to event catering — weddings, corporate boxes, and kids parties. Our signature Red Velvet became a bestseller.

2024

Modern Studio

Relocated to a bright Kiambu kitchen with larger capacity. Now offering online pre-orders & same-day pickup slots.

🧁 What we stand for since 2019

Cupcake City is a Nairobi-based cupcake studio. We bake fresh daily and take pre-orders for events like birthdays, weddings, office parties, and baby showers.

🍰 Fresh ingredients

We focus on flavour first — balanced sweetness and soft crumb. Real butter, farm-fresh eggs, no shortcuts.

🎉 Event-ready

We offer clean, elegant styles plus fun kid-friendly sets. Customise colours, toppers and flavours.

📱 Fast checkout

Add to cart and checkout using WhatsApp with full order details. We'll confirm your slot within minutes.

🎨 Want a custom theme or branded cupcakes? Contact us and we’ll plan it together — from elegant monograms to cartoon characters.
